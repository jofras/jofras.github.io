About Me

I'm a recent computer science graduate from ETH Zurich, currently serving in the electronic warfare division of the Swiss Armed Forces.

During my degree, I spent a semester at Purdue University (in Indiana), where I built a graphics pipeline from scratch and raced with the triathlon club. My thesis was on word embeddings from ASR transcripts, supervised by Alexander Hoyle and Ryan Cotterell at Rycolab.

After graduating, I worked in Neuchâtel's (city in the French part of Switzerland) cantonal IT department, speeding up legacy code and building diagnostic tools for the team (and learning French along the way). This summer, I'm excited to join Universitätsmedizin Mannheim for an internship in medical technology.

Next year, I want to start studying neuroscience in the IPN program at McGill University. Long-term, I'd love to work at the intersection of language, brain, and computation.
